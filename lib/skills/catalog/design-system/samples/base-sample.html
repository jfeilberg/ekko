/02The contract
What a brand sets

Four moves turn Base into any brand.

  1. 01

    Colour.

    Map the warm-grey ramp onto your palette. Set one accent to leave monochrome behind.

  2. 02

    Type.

    Swap the Inter faces for your own. Display and body are two roles, one or two families.

  3. 03

    Logo.

    Drop an SVG lockup in, or keep the plain text wordmark. Both are wired already.

  4. 04

    Nothing else.

    The engine and every slide primitive re-skin with no other change.

cp -r brands/base brands/<newbrand>, then edit one file.

/04The shift
Fallback · Base

No brand linked

An implicit fallback.

  • System fonts that render differently on every machine.
  • A warning in the validator, not a choice.
  • Nothing to point a deck or a fork at.

Base

A deliberate default.

  • Self-hosted fonts, identical in browser and PDF.
  • A real, linkable, validator-clean pack.
  • The documented scaffold every new brand starts from.
Emphasis here is italic and weight only. No colour. That is what keeps it unbranded.
